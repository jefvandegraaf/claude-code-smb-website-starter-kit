- **Water heater replacement:** tank and tankless; same-day in most cases; typical $1,800–$3,400 installed; fixes "no hot water before work".
- **Drain cleaning and camera inspection:** kitchen, bathroom, main line; $189–$450; fixes recurring backups.
- **Galvanized pipe replacement:** partial or whole-house; quoted after inspection, typically $3,500–$9,000; fixes low pressure and rusty water.
- **Emergency repairs:** burst pipes, leaks, no water; 24/7; call-out $175 plus repair.
